Tournament Rules

Play fair, respect opponents, and enjoy the game.

  1. Each team must register before the registration deadline.
  2. Match format and overs will be confirmed by organizers.
  3. Players must follow the umpire's decisions.
  4. Teams should report to the venue before match time.
  5. In case of a tie, the organizers will announce the tie-break rules.
  6. Any disputes will be resolved by the tournament committee.
  7. Organizers reserve the right to modify the schedule when necessary.
